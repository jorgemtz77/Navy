Jorge Tadillo Martínez | Military Experience

1. What is the Heroica Escuela Naval Militar (HENM)?

Officer commissioning academy of the Mexican Navy. A military higher education institution (university) developing leaders under a strict boarding regime.

Selection Funnel

Annual applicants ~8,000 applicants nationwide.
Admitted cadets 300 enter the academy (acceptance rate: 3.75%).
Graduates after 5 years 148 graduate (completion rate: 49.3% relative to intake).
Cumulative graduation probability 1.85% of total applicants. Fewer than 2 out of 100 complete the program.
