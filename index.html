1. What is the Heroica Escuela Naval Militar (HENM)?

Officer commissioning academy of the Mexican Navy. A military higher education institution (university) developing leaders under a strict boarding regime.

Selection Funnel

Annual applicants ~40,000 applicants nationwide.
Admitted cadets 300 enter the academy (acceptance rate: 0.75%).
Graduates after 5 years 148 graduate (completion rate: 49.3% relative to intake).
Cumulative graduation probability 0.37% of total applicants. Fewer than 1 out of 100 complete the program.

3. Trajectory

“Character is not built; it is forged.”

After 5 years under a military routine that demands reaching your mental and physical limits to meet academic and physical standards without neglecting military duties, I learned that:

  • Suffering is a constant, not the exception.
  • The objective is achieved at all costs.
  • Cooperation is fundamental to accomplish the mission.
  • Success demands sacrifice.
